Instrument samples in this folder (banjo, acoustic_guitar_steel, violin, flute) are from the
FluidR3_GM General MIDI soundfont by Frank Wen, rendered to per-note mp3 files by the
midi-js-soundfonts project (Benjamin Gleitzman, https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts).

FluidR3_GM is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 3.0:
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/

Files were renamed to their MIDI note numbers; the audio is unchanged.
